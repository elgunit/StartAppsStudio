---
name: Resources hub direction
description: Visual and information-architecture direction for the public Resources page.
---

The Resources page should read as a product-decision hub: lead with a visual route map, expose section navigation, and give routes, packages, tools, and Journal notes clearly different section treatments.

**Why:** A uniform sequence of headlines, cards, and article links made the page feel like a generic coding-news or blog page even though its content is meant to help founders choose what to build.

**How to apply:** Preserve strong scan paths and product-oriented comparison patterns when adding resources. Do not let new content turn every section into another identical article-card grid.
