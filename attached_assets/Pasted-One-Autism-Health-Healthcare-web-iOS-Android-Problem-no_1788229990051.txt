One Autism Health — Healthcare, web + iOS + Android

Problem: no way for families to find local providers

Families were searching a global database and getting results that meant
nothing where they actually lived. We needed localised search on live data
across web and both mobile platforms, and we needed it built properly the
first time because of who uses it. The MVP was live in three months and we
were at 10,000 users six months after that.

Founder, autism care platform  ·  MVP in 3 months · 10K users in 6 months

Wivity — Connectivity, brand + web

Problem: no cohesive brand identity

We had a product and no visual language holding it together, which was a
problem the moment anyone serious looked at us. The identity was designed in
Figma and the site was built and live in under a month. The consortium
acquisition came through the following week.

Founder, connectivity company  ·  Design to shipped in 3 months

A note on that one: I've written "the acquisition came through the following week" as sequence, not cause. Worth keeping it that way — the timing is impressive on its own and claiming credit for an acquisition is the kind of thing that reads as overreach to anyone who's been through one.

Noiseaware — SaaS, UI/UX overhaul

Problem: incomplete interface, broken user flows

The product worked but the experience had gaps nobody had mapped, and users
were falling into flows that just ended. The overhaul went through every
state, including the ones we'd never designed for. Three to four months and
we had an interface that matched what the product could actually do.

Product lead, property monitoring platform  ·  Full UI/UX redesign in 3-4 months

Opensend — SaaS, CRM + email platform

Problem: no CRM dashboard, no intake system

We were running email blasts with nothing behind them. No dashboard, no
intake, no way for a client to manage their own sends. Five months later we
had email push management, per-client customisation, and every flow designed
end to end.

Founder, email platform  ·  Built in 5 months
