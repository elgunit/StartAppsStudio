---
name: Project mockup content policy
description: Keep showcase visuals realistic and product-specific without revealing or inventing client branding.
---

Project showcase artwork should resemble a credible working product for its case-study category, using screens, data, controls, maps, tables, and device views as appropriate. Do not add a product name, wordmark, logo, or invented client identity inside the visual.

**Why:** The founder wants the work to feel real and tailored while keeping client branding private and the portfolio adaptable.

**How to apply:** Use unbranded, interface-level details and neutral functional labels only. Keep the project’s descriptive identity in the surrounding case-study copy and accessible image alt text, not inside the artwork.
