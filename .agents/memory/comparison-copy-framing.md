---
name: Comparison copy framing
description: The durable messaging principle for comparison modals and adjacent landing-page copy.
---

Frame comparisons around the client's finish line: what they can review during delivery, own at handoff, operate after launch, and pass to another team. Use practical due-diligence questions instead of adversarial “we do this, competitors do that” claims.

**Why:** Vendor-versus-vendor language felt childish and weakened the more valuable benefit: the client receives a coherent product, clear ownership, understandable costs, and a usable handoff.

**How to apply:** When revising comparison cards, pricing explanations, AI-efficiency claims, or delivery messaging, lead with client decisions and outcomes. Use neutral labels such as “questions worth asking” and describe managed delivery through accountability, visibility, continuity, and transferability.
