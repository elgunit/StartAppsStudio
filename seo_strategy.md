# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing homepage (`/`)
- Journal index (`/journal`)
- Journal article pages (`/journal/:slug`)
- Crawlability files (`/robots.txt`, `/sitemap.xml`, `/llms.txt`, `/llms-full.txt`)

## Out of scope
- Authenticated app screens and dashboards served by the Expo app
- Admin endpoints under `/api/admin/**`
- Internal API endpoints under `/api/**` except SEO crawler-facing text/XML routes

- Early-stage founders looking for MVP design and development help
- Non-technical founders evaluating mockup, prototype, and MVP partners
